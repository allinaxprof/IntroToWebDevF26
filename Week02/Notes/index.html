Pancakes

These pancakes use an interesting buttermilk alternative - vinegar and milk. I promise, you can't taste the vinegar.

Ingredients

  • Dry Ingredients
    • 1 1/2 cup - Flour
    • 3 1/2 tsp - Baking Powder
    • 1 tbsp - Sugar
    • 1/4 tsp (a pinch) - Salt
  • Wet Ingredients
    • 1 1/4 cup - Milk
    • 1 tbsp - Vinegar
    • 1 - Egg (large)
    • 3 tbsp - Butter (melted)

Directions

  1. Add the vinegar to the milk, mix, and set aside
  2. Combine evenly and sift flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt together
  3. Combine evenly whisked egg, milk mixture, and melted butter
  4. Pour wet mixture into dry and mix until smooth
  5. Heat a lightly oiled skillet over medium-high heat
  6. Pour portions of about 1/4 cup of batter on the skillet
  7. Wait about 2-3 minutes until the edges of the pancakes are dry (bubbles are also a good sign)
  8. Flip each pancake and leave until cooked through (about 1-2 minutes)

I'm not sure where the original recipe came from, but my family has been making it for years.
